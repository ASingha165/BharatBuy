## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an authoritative, analytical, and forensic enterprise standard built for Indian industrial procurement leadership, supply chain heads, and technical compliance auditors. It rejects consumer retail or marketplace idioms in favor of high-density institutional clarity. The interface communicates statutory rigor, verifiable evidentiary backing, and high-stakes operational intelligence.

The visual style synthesizes **Modern Enterprise Precision** and **Technical Utilitarianism**:
- **High-Density Legibility:** Compact layouts optimized for multi-attribute specifications, bill of materials (BOM) cross-referencing, and multi-tier vendor evaluation.
- **Evidentiary Hierarchy:** Distinct visual weights for statutory standards compliance (Bureau of Indian Standards - BIS, ISO, ASTM), material test certificates (MTCs), and confidence thresholds.
- **Measured Restraint:** Saffron and amber accents denote actionable alerts and verification prerequisites, balanced by structural slate tones and unambiguous emerald validation states.

## Colors

The color palette reflects institutional authority, statutory compliance, and rigorous data density.

### Primary & Action Layers
- **Primary Cobalt (`#1E40AF` / `#2563EB`):** Dedicated to definitive system actions, primary workflows, active navigation states, and confirmed procurement actions.
- **Deep Slate Anchors (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`):** Establishes structural dominance across persistent top-level navigation, command bars, table header clusters, and analytical sidebar framing.
- **Surface Neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`):** Crisp, low-fatigue slate-tinted canvas and container layers engineered for continuous operational shifts.

### Regulatory & Evidentiary States
- **Statutory Verified / Matched (`#059669` / Surface `#ECFDF5` / Border `#A7F3D0`):** Signifies absolute BIS conformity, active license validation, and 100% evidentiary proof.
- **Requires Live Verification (`#D97706` / Surface `#FFFBEB` / Border `#FDE68A`):** Used with extreme discipline to flag expiring certifications, required physical lab tests, or impending standard changes (e.g., QCO revisions).
- **Insufficient Evidence / Non-Compliant (`#DC2626` / Surface `#FEF2F2` / Border `#FECACA`):** Unambiguous audit failures, lapsed vendor licenses, and structural mismatches.
- **Informational / Neutral Metric (`#64748B` / Surface `#F8FAFC` / Border `#CBD5E1`):** Metadata, secondary telemetry, and standard categorization tags.

## Typography

Typography enforces a strict dichotomy between human-readable analytical narrative and machine-verifiable technical data.

- **Primary Typeface (`Inter`):** Applied to headlines, analytical narratives, executive digests, and conversational support panels. Letter spacing is slightly cinched in headline levels to preserve horizontal density on multi-column split views.
- **Monospaced Technical Typeface (`JetBrains Mono`):** Non-negotiable for all Indian Standard codes (`IS 2062`, `IS 7098 (Part 1)`), material grade classifications (`Fe500D`, `SS 316L`), GSTINs, CML certificate IDs, test report serials, unit pricing, and tabular ledgers.
- **Information Hierarchy:** Headers avoid decorative scaling, capping at 28px for root dashboard views to ensure primary data stays above the display fold across standard enterprise laptops (1366x768 to 1920x1080).

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is structured on a rigorous, compact **12-column grid layout** utilizing a baseline 4px step scale, optimized for high data density without visual clutter.

- **Split-Panel Shell Architecture:**
  - **Global Navigation:** 64px fixed top header with structural navigation anchored in `#0F172A`.
  - **Faceted Audit Rail (Left):** Collapsible 320px (`20rem`) panel housing multi-tier filters (BIS mandatory compliance, CML active status, freight proximity radius, manufacturing capability audit scores).
  - **Analytical Workspace (Center):** Fluid dynamic grid for comparison matrixes, material specification sheets, and geographic supply hubs.
  - **Intelligence & Evidence Drawer (Right):** Collapsible 360px contextual panel displaying original scanned MTCs, laboratory test audits, and AI provenance citations.
- **Density Standards:** Form controls and table row heights are condensed: table rows use 36px (compact) to 44px (default standard) cell heights. Card interiors adhere strictly to `space-md` (12px) or `space-lg` (16px) margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies primarily on **low-contrast structural borders** and **tonal surface containment** rather than decorative multi-layered drop shadows.

- **Surface Layers:**
  - Base Shell: `#F8FAFC`
  - Active Work Containers / Cards: `#FFFFFF`
  - Nested Sub-Panels / Table Headers: `#F1F5F9`
  - Inverted Control Clusters: `#0F172A` and `#1E293B`
- **Borders & Dividers:** 1px hairline rules using `#E2E8F0` for interior dividers, stepping to `#CBD5E1` for actionable component edges and structural card boundaries.
- **Elevation System:**
  - **Level 0 (Flat):** Tables, form inputs, inline data badges, and status panels use static 1px borders with zero elevation.
  - **Level 1 (Card Default):** `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` paired with `#E2E8F0` hairline border.
  - **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Menus):** `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` for dropdown pickers, popovers, and verification evidence popups.
  - **Level 3 (Inspection Modals / Scanned Document Viewer):** `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a 40% `#0F172A` backdrop tint.

## Shapes

The design system specifies **Level 1 (Soft)** corner geometry, signaling engineering precision, stability, and corporate utility.

- **Standard Inputs, Badges, & Buttons:** 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius. Rounded corners are intentional micro-accents rather than soft consumer pills.
- **Analytical Cards, Comparison Modules, & Data Tables:** 4px (`0.25rem`) to 6px outer boundary radius. Inner table cells maintain square 0px internal framing.
- **Technical Tags & Badges:** Strictly 2px to 4px radius. Circular shapes are permitted exclusively for vendor status indicators (8px status dots) and user profile avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E40AF`, hover `#1D4ED8`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius. Uses `Inter` SemiBold (13px/18px) with 8px 14px padding. Height 36px.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, hover `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive / Flag:** Background `#FEF2F2`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`, text `#DC2626`, hover `#FEE2E2`.
- **Tertiary Utility:** Ghost appearance, text `#475569`, hover `#F1F5F9`.

### Status & Compliance Badges
Badges are non-pill, compact tags with 1px structural borders and fixed 2px border radius, featuring leading status indicators:
- **Ready with Verification:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, `#A7F3D0` border. Prefix icon: solid checkmark shield.
- **Requires Live Verification:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text, `#FDE68A` border. Prefix icon: alert triangle.
- **Insufficient Evidence:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, `#FECACA` border. Prefix icon: slashed eye or exclamation.
- **BIS Standard Tag:** `#F1F5F9` background, `#1E293B` text, `#CBD5E1` border, set strictly in `JetBrains Mono` 11px.

### Form Inputs & Multi-Select Dropdowns
- **Text & Numeric Inputs:** Height 34px, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`. Focus state applies a 1px border `#1E40AF` with an explicit 2px focus ring `#DBEAFE`.
- **Monospace Code Input:** Applied to GSTIN, CML, and SAC/HSN queries.
- **Industrial Specification Selectors:** Compact pill-less toggle chips with 1px borders, highlighting selected parameters in deep slate (`#0F172A`) with white text.

### Data Tables & Comparison Grids
- **Header Cells:** Background `#F8FAFC`, border-bottom 2px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#475569` in uppercase `Inter` 11px/14px (letter-spacing: 0.05em).
- **Data Rows:** Background alternate zebra striping (`#FFFFFF` to `#FBFCFD`), border-bottom 1px solid `#F1F5F9`. Hover row transitions to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Technical Columns:** Right-aligned tabular monospaced numbers for thickness (mm), yield strength (MPa), tolerance values, and pricing per metric ton (₹/MT).

### Cards & Evidence Drawers
- **Specification Match Cards:** Pure white container, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, left vertical accent border (3px) indicating standard match confidence: Emerald (100% statutory match), Amber (provisional match), or Slate (unverified).
- **Evidence Drawer:** Structured layout presenting side-by-side extracted AI parameters versus raw PDF MTC documents, with bounding box highlighting linked to citation labels.
